BUILT FOR SPEED

Less pipeline. Less waiting.

Dayaah Capture skips the stuff you do not need when the goal is simple: get HDMI video onto your screen as quickly as possible.

01

Newest frame wins

A single-frame strategy avoids building a long video queue behind what is happening on the console right now.

010203NOW
02

Raw video modes

Direct NV12 and YUY2 capture through Windows Media Foundation, with no hidden MJPEG or H.264 recompression stage.

NV12YUY2RAW
03

D3D11 presentation

Hardware video processing, scaling and a flip-discard swap chain keep the display path lean.

04

Audio in the same app

WASAPI monitoring with selectable gain from 0 to +18 dB. No separate player process required.

05

Tearing or VSync. Your call.

Use minimum-latency presentation for speed, or optional VSync when a clean image matters more than one refresh interval.

LOW LATENCYVSYNC
06

Tiny, local and boring—in a good way

No installer, account, ads, telemetry, analytics or network connection required to use the app.

COMPATIBILITY

What you need.

Dayaah Capture is intentionally picky about video formats because predictable latency is the whole point.

  • ✓
    Windows 10 or 11 x64Native desktop application.
  • ✓
    Raw NV12 / YUY2Your capture card must expose one of these modes through Media Foundation.
  • ✓
    Windows audio inputRequired only if you want audio monitoring.
