All notable changes to Dayaah Capture are documented here.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-08

### Added

- Native Media Foundation capture for raw NV12 and YUY2 video.
- Low-latency D3D11 rendering with a newest-frame-only queue.
- Optional VSync limited to a maximum one-frame queue.
- WASAPI audio capture and playback in the same process.
- Selectable digital audio gain from 0 to +18 dB.
- Automatic device and raw video-mode enumeration.
- Fullscreen mode, mute shortcut, automatic cursor hiding, and persistent settings.
- English and Spanish project documentation.
